Front Porch

Every new tab, a moving scene

A clock, Google search with everything the real homepage has, your shortcuts and the whole Pixel Americana library, animated. The demo below is the real thing: click around.

New Tab
Chrome · Edge · Brave · Arczip Firefoxzip Open full screen
  • Google, the whole barGmail, Images, the apps grid and your account in the top right, like google.com.
  • Search toolsVoice search, Google Lens (drop, paste or upload an image) and an AI Mode button, right in the search box.
  • Suggestions as you typeLive Google suggestions with your completion in bold, plus your recent searches. Arrow keys work.
  • Your shortcutsYour most-visited sites as tiles under the search box. Hover to remove one; hide them all from the bottom bar.
  • Type a URLAnything that looks like an address opens directly. Press / to jump to the search box.
  • Scene galleryEvery scene, animated. Categories, search and favorites; press G to open it.

Install

  1. Download the zip for your browser, unzip it and move the folder somewhere it can stay (not Downloads).
  2. Go to chrome://extensions (edge://extensions in Edge) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder. Open a new tab and choose Keep it if asked.
  4. Updating: replace the folder's files and click the reload arrow on the extension's card.

Chrome will list what the extension can do: read your top sites (for the shortcut tiles), see your signed-in email (for the letter in the account circle; nothing is sent anywhere) and reach Google's suggestion service. Firefox: about:debugging › This Firefox › Load Temporary Add-on. Safari: set https://pixelamericana.github.io/newtab/ as your homepage.
